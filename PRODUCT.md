# DonorNet

## Register

product

## Users

People who donate blood, people trying to find compatible donors, and healthcare or blood-bank teams coordinating urgent requests. Donors often use DonorNet in short, mobile-first moments: checking eligibility, finding a nearby center, responding to an emergency, or managing an appointment.

## Product Purpose

DonorNet connects donors, recipients, hospitals, and blood banks so people can find the right next action quickly. Success means a donor can understand their eligibility, trust the urgency of a request, and complete a useful action with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, human, quietly optimistic. The voice is warm and direct, celebrating impact without gamifying a serious health workflow. The interface should create confidence in routine moments and clarity during emergencies.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy charity dashboards, guilt-heavy emergency messaging, game-like streak pressure, and novelty interactions that compete with the donation task. Avoid generic saturated gradients, dense admin tables, and decorative motion that does not explain state or give feedback.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the next useful action, especially when a donor is eligible or a request is urgent.
- Make impact tangible through concise progress, human language, and trustworthy status cues.
- Use urgency responsibly: direct, specific, and paired with a clear action.
- Keep the system calm by default, with delight reserved for meaningful moments and confirmations.
- Build confidence through predictable navigation, accessible targets, and fast feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light and dark themes, dynamic text, reduced motion, and color-independent status communication. Maintain at least 44pt touch targets and readable contrast for body text. Pair semantic colors with labels or icons so urgency and eligibility are never conveyed by color alone.
